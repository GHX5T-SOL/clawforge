## New Brand Decision
- **Name:** `NexaPersona Labs`
- **Tagline:** `Autonomous Influence Infrastructure`
- **Rationale:** stronger enterprise trust signal, less polarizing than prior naming, scalable to platform/product evolution.

## Unified Strategy Thesis
Build an autonomous influencer operating system where each AI influencer is a persistent OpenClaw sub-agent with:
- daily trend research,
- script and asset generation,
- scheduled publishing,
- engagement handling,
- KPI feedback loops.

The business is not just content production; it is **orchestration infrastructure + monetizable persona portfolio**.

## Business Model
- **70%** sponsored campaign execution
- **20%** retainer-based influencer-as-a-service
- **10%** premium subscription channels (policy-partitioned)

## 180-Day Operating Plan

### 0–30 days
- Legal setup, contracts, disclosure framework
- OpenClaw environment and secure key management
- First persona cohort + quality system
- Pilot outbound to first 50 brand prospects

### 31–90 days
- 8–12 active personas
- 3–10 paid pilot campaigns
- KPI baseline instrumentation (engagement, conversion, CAC, payback)
- Weekly execution rhythm established

### 91–180 days
- 20+ personas across niche clusters
- Repeatable campaign delivery pipeline
- Dedicated ad budget allocator and deal pipeline governance
- Investor-grade reporting + dataroom

## Technical Blueprint
- **Orchestrator:** OpenClaw on Mac Mini / Studio
- **Generation:** Flux.1 + Kling + Runway + ElevenLabs
- **Distribution:** Instagram Graph API + TikTok Business API
- **Analytics:** KPI telemetry for follower growth, ER, CVR, revenue attribution
- **Governance:** safety policy gates and disclosure audit logs

## Compliance Red Lines
- No account buying or engagement manipulation.
- No deepfake impersonation of real people.
- Mandatory disclosure protocol for sponsored posts.
- Segment-specific policy isolation for sensitive categories.

## Metrics Framework
- Revenue, margin, monthly run-rate
- Persona-level ER/CVR
- Campaign delivery SLA
- CAC/LTV and payback period
- Brand retention and expansion rate

## Funding Pathways
- **Bootstrap mode:** lean run-rate with founder-led operations.
- **Seed mode:** $500k–$1M to accelerate team, tooling, and GTM velocity.
